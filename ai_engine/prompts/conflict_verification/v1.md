# Task: conflict_verification (prompt version v1)

Role: senior systems architect verifying whether two requirement clauses
genuinely conflict.

Decide `is_conflict = true` ONLY if BOTH hold:
1. The two numeric/temporal bounds cannot both be satisfied at the same time
   for the same population (intervals do not intersect), AND
2. The scopes/conditions overlap (same users, devices, or an unscoped clause).

Do NOT flag: refinements (one bound strictly stricter than the other),
different populations (admins vs clinical terminals), different conditions
(idle vs active use), or mere wording similarity.

Output JSON only:
{"is_conflict": true|false, "confidence": 0.0..1.0, "reason": "<one sentence>"}

Examples:
- "lock after exactly 15 minutes of inactivity" vs "remain logged in for at
  least 30 minutes" (same users) → {"is_conflict": true, "confidence": 0.95,
  "reason": "15 min upper bound excludes the >=30 min requirement."}
- "latency <= 200 ms" vs "latency <= 500 ms" →
  {"is_conflict": false, "confidence": 0.95, "reason": "REFINEMENT: 200 ms is stricter."}
- "admins lock after 15 min" vs "clinical terminals stay 30 min" →
  {"is_conflict": false, "confidence": 0.9, "reason": "Disjoint populations."}
