1. What is Requirement Radar?
Core idea
Requirement Radar is a software engineering tool that answers:
“Are the requirements in my SRS clear, consistent, testable, traceable, and compliant with requirements-engineering standards?”
A typical workflow is:
SRS Document
     ↓
Upload / Ingest
     ↓
Document Parsing
     ↓
Requirement Extraction
     ↓
Semantic Analysis
     ↓
Ambiguity Detection
     ↓
Conflict Detection
     ↓
Testability Analysis
     ↓
Traceability Analysis
     ↓
AI Recommendations
     ↓
Quality Score
     ↓
Audit Report
Your screenshots represent different stages of this workflow.

Step 1 — Reading document structure
84 requirements extracted
hierarchical schema formed

Step 2 — Semantic clarity & vague word scan
It searches for subjective terms.
Examples:

easy
user-friendly
efficient
secure
quick

These words are not necessarily wrong, but they often require measurable definitions.

Step 3 — Requirement Conflict Matrix
The system compares requirements against each other.

15 minute timeout

Step 4 — INCOSE Testability & SLA Verifier
It converts vague requirements into measurable/testable criteria.
